Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so you cancel in your Apple settings:

  1. On your iPhone open Settings and tap your name (Apple ID).
  2. Tap Subscriptions and choose Abby.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your Abby account does not cancel your subscription.

For a refund, request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Abby, go to the paywall or Profile, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are using the same Apple ID you bought with. If your purchase does not appear, email support@abby.fitness and we will help.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: go to Profile > Privacy > Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account and all your photos. Backups are purged within 30 days.

By email: write to support@abby.fitness or privacy@abby.fitness with the subject “Delete my account”. Because the app has no email login, the quickest way is to delete in the app; if you write to us we may need details to confirm it is you, and we will confirm once it is done.

To delete only a scan or photo, open it in the app and choose delete. To get a copy of your data first, use Profile > Privacy.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Why did my scan not work, and what makes a good photo?

A scan can fail if the photo is unclear or the midsection is not visible. For the best result:

  • Stand in front of a mirror or have the phone at waist height, facing you straight on.
  • Use bright, even light, ideally from in front, and avoid strong shadows.
  • Wear fitted clothing or workout wear, and keep your midsection fully in frame.
  • Stand relaxed and upright, with arms away from your midsection.
  • Hold the phone steady and keep the camera lens clean.
  • Keep to one person in the photo: yourself.

If it still does not work, check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email support@abby.fitness with the time it happened.

Tips for automatic rep counting
  • Prop your phone steady, about two to three metres away, so your whole body is in frame.
  • Use good lighting and a clear background, with space around you.
  • Wear fitted clothing so your movement is easy to see.
  • Use the angle shown in the exercise video.
  • Move through the full range of the exercise at a steady pace.

Rep counting runs on your phone and your workout video is never recorded or uploaded. You can always count reps yourself if you prefer.

Why am I not getting notifications?
  1. On your iPhone open Settings > Notifications > Abby and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  2. Check that Focus modes or Scheduled Summary are not hiding them.
  3. Check the reminder time and days in Abby’s settings.
  4. If it still does not work, restart the app, then email support@abby.fitness.

You can turn reminders off at any time in iPhone Settings.

How do I export or correct my data?

Go to Profile > Privacy to export your data, and edit your details in your profile. See the Privacy Policy for all your rights.

Is Abby medical advice?

No. Abby gives fitness guidance only. Scores are visual guidance, not body-composition or medical measurements, and the AI scan is not a diagnosis. See the Terms of Use.
