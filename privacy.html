1. Who we are

Abby (the iPhone app) and abby.fitness (our website) are provided by Dario Bek, based in the United Kingdom (“Abby”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach our privacy team at privacy@abby.fitness.

This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, so we have kept it plain.

3. Your photos

  • Scan photo. You take one front photo of your midsection. It is sent securely to our AI provider to produce your result.
  • Metadata removed. EXIF and location metadata are stripped from the photo.
  • Original deleted. The original photo is deleted right after analysis.
  • Kept only if you choose. A re-encoded copy is stored only if you choose “keep my photos to track progress”. It is encrypted, private, and only you can view it.
  • You are in control. You can delete any scan or photo at any time in the app. Stored photos are removed after 365 days at most.
  • Never used to train AI. Your photos are not used to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s.

4. AI processing

To analyse your scan photo we use Claude, a vision model made by Anthropic. Your photo (with metadata removed) is sent to Anthropic over an encrypted connection for the sole purpose of producing your result, and the result is returned to us. Anthropic acts as our service provider for this purpose.

The result is visual guidance generated by an AI. It is not a medical assessment, not a diagnosis, and not a measurement of body composition or health. AI output can be imperfect, and you can rescan or contact us if something looks off. We do not use your scan to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers are located in, or process data in, countries other than your own, which may have different data protection rules. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we do so using safeguards recognised by applicable law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision where one exists. Our main hosting location is Frankfurt, Germany (EU). You can contact us at privacy@abby.fitness for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data (profile, scan results, workout logs): until you delete your account.
  • Photos: the scan original is deleted right after analysis. Photos you chose to keep are removed when you delete them, when you delete your account, or after 365 days at most.
  • Backups: deleted data is purged from backups within 30 days of deletion.
  • Billing records: kept for as long as the law requires. These are held by Apple and our payment tools, and we keep only what we need to show your purchase status.
  • Support messages: kept while we help you and for a reasonable period afterwards, or until you ask us to delete them, unless we must keep them by law.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit. Sensitive fields (such as your health answers and weight) are encrypted at rest, and stored photos are encrypted and private to your account. Access to personal data is limited to what is needed to run the service. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we work to keep your information safe and will tell you and the relevant authorities about a data breach where the law requires it.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy (export);
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your data;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew);
  • object to or ask us to restrict certain processing, and, where it applies, receive your data in a portable format;
  • not be discriminated against for using your privacy rights.

How to do it

  • Export your data: in the app, go to Profile > Privacy.
  • Correct your details: edit your profile in the app, or email us.
  • Withdraw a consent: in the app under Profile > Privacy (for example analytics, keeping photos), or by email.
  • Delete a scan or photo: in the app, on the scan or photo itself.
  • Delete your whole account and all photos: Profile > Privacy > Delete account.
  • Anything else: email privacy@abby.fitness. We will reply within one month, or tell you if we need longer as the law allows. We may need to confirm it is you first.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. See our Terms of Use and Support page.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please talk to us first, and you also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator (for example the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK, or your local EU supervisory authority). California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

10. Children

Abby is for adults aged 18 and over only. We check your date of birth in onboarding and again on our server. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, please email privacy@abby.fitness and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and if a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email. Where the law requires your consent for a new use of your data, we will ask for it.
